# Part 3 — Post-conversation questions

## 3.1 Post-conversation questionnaire

*One page, immediately after the conversation and before any video. Item order
is fixed, not randomized, so it matches the paper version.*

**Page header (stays pinned at the top while the page scrolls):**

> Thinking back on the conversation you just had, please answer the following.

**Each of the following is a 0–10 scale.** The circles are numbered 0 to 10,
with **Not at all** under the 0 and **Very much** under the 10.

1. To what extent do you feel YOU were influencing YOUR PARTNER'S emotions?
2. To what extent do you feel YOUR PARTNER was influencing YOUR emotions?
3. To what extent do you feel you could predict YOUR PARTNER'S emotions?
4. How well did the conversation "flow"?
5. How much did you enjoy the conversation you had with your study partner?
6. Can you picture your study partner fitting in with your friends?
7. How much would you like to be friends with your study partner?
8. How comfortable did you feel during the conversation?
9. How much were you focused on yourself during the conversation?

**Last item, on its own −5 to +5 scale:**

> Think about how much you and your study partner each talked during your
> conversation, and indicate your relative contributions on the scale below:

Anchors, left to right:

| Position | Label |
|---|---|
| −5 | My study partner spoke much more than I did |
| 0 | My study partner and I spoke the same amount |
| +5 | I spoke much more than my study partner did |

`[Continue]` sits in the bottom-right corner.

# Part 4 — Affective tracking task (watching your conversation)

## 4.1 Select video file

*RA-facing. The RA loads the recording of this dyad's conversation.*

> **Select Video File**
>
> _(a file picker accepting .mp4 and .mov)_

If the wrong file type is chosen:

> Please select a valid MP4 or MOV video file.

## 4.2 Instructions

*Five screens. Text accumulates four to a screen, then the page clears. Any key
advances; `[← Previous]` appears in the top-left from the second screen on.*

1. In this part of the study, you will watch the video recording of the
   conversation you just had.
2. We are interested in two things:
   1. How YOU were feeling during the conversation.
   2. How YOUR PARTNER was feeling during the conversation.
3. The video is split into parts. Before each part, the screen will tell you
   whether to focus on YOUR OWN feelings or YOUR PARTNER'S feelings, and a
   reminder stays in the corner of the screen while you watch.
4. As the video plays, continuously move the slider to indicate how positive or
   negative YOU or YOUR PARTNER felt at that moment during the conversation.
5. At certain points, you will be asked to write a short response and make
   ratings about how you or your partner felt during the part of the
   conversation you just watched.

Footer on every instruction screen:

> Press any key to continue

## 4.3 Perspective announcement

*Before each of the four blocks. The screen holds for 3 seconds before any key
will advance it. Randy, 2026-08-05: the same wait screen, in the same words, as
the one between the two perspectives of the video task (5.3).*

> You will now be rating **YOUR OWN FEELINGS** during the conversation.
> _(partner blocks:)_ You will now be rating **YOUR PARTNER'S FEELINGS** during the conversation.
>
> Think about how **YOU** were feeling, moment to moment.
> _(partner blocks:)_ Think about how **YOUR PARTNER** was feeling, moment to moment.
>
> Continuing in {n}…
>
> Press any key to continue _(appears once the countdown reaches zero)_

*Which perspective a participant starts on is decided by the parity of their
study ID — odd IDs (the left seat) start with their own feelings, even IDs (the
right seat) start with their partner's — so the two members of a dyad are never
rating the same target over the same stretch of the conversation.*

## 4.4 Watching, with the continuous slider

*The video plays. The mouse controls the slider; the pointer is hidden.*

Prompt above the video:

> How positive or negative did **YOU** feel during this moment in the conversation?
>
> _(partner blocks:)_ How positive or negative did **YOUR PARTNER** feel during this moment in the conversation?

Reminder box, top-right, on screen for the whole block:

> YOU ARE RATING
> **YOUR OWN FEELINGS**
> _(partner blocks:)_ **YOUR PARTNER'S FEELINGS**

Slider labels, left to middle to right:

> Very Negative &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Neutral &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Very Positive

## 4.5 Writing + elicitation rating

*The video pauses every 2.5 minutes for this screen, and it appears once more
when the video ends.*

Only on the screen after the video has finished:

> LAST PART OF THE VIDEO

Then:

> Please use the box below to write about how **YOU were** feeling during the
> part of the conversation you just watched.
>
> _(partner blocks:)_ Please use the box below to write about how
> **YOUR PARTNER was** feeling during the part of the conversation you just watched.
>
> _(text box placeholder)_ Type your response here...

Then the 1–9 rating:

> To what extent do you feel that **YOUR PARTNER** elicited these feelings in
> **YOU**? Click the corresponding number.
>
> _(partner blocks:)_ To what extent do you feel that **YOU** elicited these
> feelings in **YOUR PARTNER**? Click the corresponding number.

The scale runs **Not at all** on the left, then circles numbered 1 to 9, then
**Very** on the right. Under it:

> Selected: {number, or — if nothing is chosen yet}

Footer:

> Press `Tab` or click Continue.

`[Continue]` sits in the bottom-right corner.

*This whole cycle — 4.3, 4.4, 4.5 — runs four times.*

# Part 5 — Video affective-response task

*Eight film clips. Each is watched once, then rated twice: once for the
participant and once for their partner. Which of the two comes first is drawn
separately for every clip.*

*Restructured to Randy's specification of 2026-08-05. The average-UW-student
perspective, the three-passes-over-the-clips structure and the 1–100 sliders are
all gone.*

## 5.1 Instructions

*Six screens on one page, any key to advance.*

1. In this part of the study, you will watch a series of short videos.
2. After each video, you will answer the same questions twice: once about how
   YOU felt while watching it, and once about how YOUR PARTNER would feel while
   watching it.
3. A screen before each set of questions will tell you which of the two you are
   answering. Please read it — it changes from video to video.
4. Every question is answered on a scale from 1 (Not at all) to 7 (Extremely).
5. Please watch each video all the way through. You can replay it on the
   question pages at any time.
6. We ask that you answer each question efficiently in order to keep your
   participation time within one hour.

Footer:

> Press any key to continue

## 5.2 Watch the clip

**Page header:**

> Please watch the following video all the way through.

On the page:

> Video {n} of 8

Over the video before it starts:

> **Play video**
>
> The clip plays once through. You cannot fast-forward.

Over the video after a viewing:

> **Watch again**

Under the video after a viewing:

> Viewing complete. You may watch it again, or continue.

If the clip will not load:

> This clip could not be loaded.
>
> Please let the researcher know. (Missing file, or the stimulus folder is not
> set on this machine.)

Bottom-right, until the clip has been watched:

> Please watch the video before continuing. `[Continue]` _(disabled)_

## 5.3 Which perspective comes next

*Between the clip and each set of questions, and again between the two sets.
Holds for 3 seconds before any key will advance it. Same screen, same words, as
the one in the conversation-slider task (4.3).*

> You will now be reporting how **YOU** felt.
>
> _(the other one:)_ You will now be reporting how **YOUR PARTNER** would feel.
>
> Continuing in {n}…
>
> Press any key to continue _(appears once the countdown reaches zero)_

## 5.4 Rate the clip

*The clip is on the page, at the same size on both perspectives, and can be
replayed from there. Three questions, one per emotion for that clip, in a random
order held constant across the two perspectives of the same clip.*

On the page:

> Video {n} of 8 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Rating: **YOU** / **YOUR PARTNER**

**When reporting their own feelings**, one question per emotion:

> To what extent did you feel {emotion} while watching this video?
>
> 1 … 7, anchored **Not at all** (under 1) and **Extremely** (under 7)

**When reporting their partner's:**

> To what extent would your partner feel {emotion} while watching this video?
>
> 1 … 7, anchored **Not at all** and **Extremely**

*and then, once, at the bottom of the partner page only:*

> How confident are you in your ratings of your partner?
>
> 1 … 7, anchored **Not at all** and **Extremely**

Bottom-right: `[Continue]`

*5.2 → 5.3 → 5.4 → 5.3 → 5.4 repeats for all eight clips.*
