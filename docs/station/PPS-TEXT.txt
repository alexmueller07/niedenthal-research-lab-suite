EVERY WORD IN THE PPS RATING STATION APP
Niedenthal Lab Suite v1.3.2 — Rating Station mode
Generated for Randy, updated 2026-09-21
================================================================================

This is every piece of text the app can put on screen, in the order a session
meets it. Question wording, button labels, placeholder text inside empty boxes,
pop-up dialogs, error messages — all of it, verbatim.

Notes on how to read it:
  * [RESEARCHER] marks a screen a participant never sees.
  * <angle brackets> mark something the app fills in at run time, e.g. <round>.
  * Pages whose questions are shuffled are marked; the items are listed in the
    order they appear in the source, not the order any one participant sees.
  * A "round" is one conversation. A participant stays at the same computer and
    does several, each with a different partner.

4. POST-CONVERSATION QUESTIONS  (every round)
================================================================================

Pinned header:

    Thinking back on the conversation you just had, please answer the
    following.

Nine questions, each on a 0–10 scale.  Scale ends: "Not at all" … "Very much".
Order is fixed, not shuffled.

    1.  To what extent do you feel YOU were influencing YOUR PARTNER'S
        emotions?
    2.  To what extent do you feel YOUR PARTNER was influencing YOUR emotions?
    3.  To what extent do you feel you could predict YOUR PARTNER'S emotions?
    4.  How well did the conversation "flow"?
    5.  How much did you enjoy the conversation you had with your study
        partner?
    6.  Can you picture your study partner fitting in with your friends?
    7.  How much would you like to be friends with your study partner?
    8.  How comfortable did you feel during the conversation?
    9.  How much were you focused on yourself during the conversation?

Tenth question, on a −5 … +5 scale:

    10. Think about how much you and your study partner each talked during your
        conversation, and indicate your relative contributions on the scale
        below:

        Left end:   My study partner spoke much more than I did
        Middle:     My study partner and I spoke the same amount
        Right end:  I spoke much more than my study partner did

    [Continue]

5. CONVERSATION RATING TASK (THE SLIDER)  (every round)
================================================================================

INSTRUCTION SCREENS — six, revealed one at a time. Any key, or the Continue
button, or a click anywhere on the screen, moves to the next one.

    1.  Before we begin: please check that the computer's volume is at a
        comfortable level. You will hear the audio from your conversation. Ask
        your researcher if you would like help adjusting it.

    2.  In this part of the study, you will watch the video recording of the
        conversation you just had.

    3.  We are interested in two things:
            1. How YOU were feeling during the conversation.
            2. How YOUR PARTNER was feeling during the conversation.

    4.  The video is split into parts. Before each part, the screen will tell
        you whether to focus on YOUR OWN feelings or YOUR PARTNER'S feelings,
        and a reminder stays in the corner of the screen while you watch.

    5.  As the video plays, continuously move the slider to indicate how
        positive or negative YOU or YOUR PARTNER felt at that moment during the
        conversation.

    6.  At certain points, you will be asked to write a short response and make
        ratings about how you or your partner felt during the part of the
        conversation you just watched.

  At the bottom of every instruction screen:

    Press any key to continue, or click Continue

6. SHORT VIDEO TASK  (every round)
================================================================================

INSTRUCTION SCREENS — six, same controls as above.

    1.  In this part of the study, you will watch a series of short videos.

    2.  After each video, you will answer the same questions twice: once about
        how YOU felt while watching it, and once about how YOUR PARTNER would
        feel while watching it.

    3.  A screen before each set of questions will tell you which of the two you
        are answering. Please read it — it changes from video to video.

    4.  Every question is answered on a scale from 1 (Not at all) to 7
        (Extremely).

    5.  Please watch each video all the way through. You can play it again
        later, while you answer the questions about it.

    6.  We ask that you answer each question efficiently in order to keep your
        participation time within one hour.

WHAT CHANGED IN v1.3.0
================================================================================

The real clips arrived (Ben, 2026-09-20). Forty Cowen & Keltner clips in five
groups of eight, and the group a participant gets is their round number: round
1 is group 1, round 5 is group 5. Nobody sees a clip twice across five rounds.
The three emotions each clip asks about are that clip's highest-rated three in
the published norms, restricted to the twelve emotions this study uses:

    amusement, anger, anxiety, awe, awkwardness, disappointment,
    disgust, fear, joy, sadness, surprise, sympathy

So a rating page now reads, for example:

    To what extent did you feel fear while watching this video?
    To what extent did you feel anxiety while watching this video?
    To what extent did you feel awe while watching this video?

Three more questionnaires were removed (Randy, 2026-09-20: everything that is
not about this partner is asked before the participant sits down):

    * Conversation experience - "How often were you thinking about the fact
      that your conversation was being video recorded?", "How comfortable did
      you feel during the conversation?", and the free-text box.
    * Demographics - age, Hispanic/Latino, race, sex, zip code.
    * Study feedback - "We're interested in hearing more about your experience
      with our study."

What a participant now does, per conversation:

    post-conversation questions -> conversation slider task ->
    short-video task -> partner ratings -> partner history -> round complete

and once, at the end of their last round, the video-sharing page.

The researcher setup screen picks the round with five buttons, R1 to R5, and
says which clip group that round will show.

WHAT CHANGED IN v1.2.0
================================================================================

Removed entirely. None of the following are asked any more:

    * Loneliness — 20 items, "How often do you feel that you lack
      companionship?" and the rest of the UCLA scale.
    * Social Connectedness — 20 items, "I feel disconnected from the world
      around me." and the rest.
    * Expressivity — 16 items, "I am an emotionally expressive person." and the
      rest.
    * Autism-spectrum quotient — 10 items, "I often notice small sounds when
      others do not" and the rest.
    * Emotion frequency — "In this part of the study, you will be asked to
      estimate how often YOU experience each of the previously seen states."
      with its 15 sliders (confident, grouchy, sad, assertive, unrestrained,
      nervous, irritable, lively, bold, talkative, satisfaction, love, contempt,
      disgust, embarrassment).

    was:  Please watch each video all the way through. You can replay it on the
          question pages at any time.
    now:  Please watch each video all the way through. You can play it again
          later, while you answer the questions about it.

Added:

    * The volume reminder, now the first instruction of the slider task.
    * "or click Continue" on every press-any-key screen, with a real button.
    * The scroll indicator.
    * The list of missed questions in the are-you-sure dialog, and the red *
      beside each of them on the page.
    * The session strip naming the colour pairing and the round.
    * Everything on the round-complete screen.

Timing:
